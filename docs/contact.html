Helpful questions

A few things visitors usually ask.

Anything comfortable. Most people dress casually.

How long is the service?

About 70 minutes including worship and teaching.

Is there a kids program?

Yes, during the 11:00 AM service, with nursery support available.

Yes. Sermons and event updates can be linked from the sermons page.
